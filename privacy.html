Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

For customers

You do not need an account to browse. We do not ask for your name, phone number or email on this website.

When you tap "Ask on WhatsApp", "Ask by email" or Telegram, you leave this site and talk to the seller directly. That conversation follows the privacy rules of that app or email service.

If you send a report about a shop, we store the reason and any details you type. We do not store your name or contact details with a report.

For sellers

When you create an account we store your email address and a protected version of your password. We also store your shop name, contact details, products and pictures. Your shop name, WhatsApp number, Telegram username and products are shown publicly after your shop is approved.

Your login is kept in your browser so you stay logged in. Log out on shared phones.

Who handles the data

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Data is stored with Supabase. Both may process technical information such as IP addresses to run their services.

Deleting your data

Sellers can ask us to delete their shop and account. Write to dagielectronics@example.com.

Prices and availability

Prices and stock are set by each seller and can change. Confirm with the seller before you pay or travel. Dagi Electronics Shop does not take payments on this website.
